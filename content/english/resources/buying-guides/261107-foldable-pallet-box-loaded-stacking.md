A box rated to hold your parts may still be unsuitable at the bottom of a loaded stack. Before you compare quotes, ask for the permitted **payload per box**, the **load each box can support from above**, and the **maximum number of loaded boxes in the intended use**. Ask for separate limits for stationary storage and transport.

## Read the rating for the exact box and lid

Some manufacturers publish different stacking factors for static and dynamic use. Ask the supplier to write out the total number of boxes, whether those boxes are loaded, and which movements their dynamic rating covers. A warehouse stacking figure alone leaves transport and moving an assembled stack unverified. [See an example of separate static and dynamic ratings](https://orbiseurope.eu/en/foldable-pallet-box-600/).

Ask for a drawing of the contact points between the base of the upper box and the top of the lower one. Identify the lid, runners, doors and latch positions included in the rating. A different lid or box with the same footprint can change where the weight is carried. If you intend to mix models, ask for written confirmation of that exact pairing and order in the stack.

## Work out the weight above the bottom box

Suppose each box contains **400 kg of parts** and weighs **50 kg including its lid and inserts**. In a stack of three identical loaded boxes, the bottom box holds 400 kg of its own payload and supports **900 kg above it** from the two upper boxes. The entire stack weighs **1,350 kg**. These are example masses for a quotation request.

Check how the supplier defines its stacking figure before comparing it with 900 kg. Some quotations express a limit as a number of tiers. Ask what gross mass per tier that number assumes, and whether the bottom box may carry its own full payload while supporting that weight. If weights vary, send the heaviest planned arrangement, with the gross mass and contents of each tier.

## Confirm the storage and shipping arrangements separately

For floor storage, give the supplier the intended stack height, dwell time, temperature and load distribution. Check the total mass and support footprint with the warehouse team. For shipping, send the intended vehicle arrangement, tier count and gross masses. Confirm restraint, clearance and handling with the responsible transport team. If the carrier needs to move a complete stack, specify that movement separately.

Ask for evidence that identifies the assembled box and test conditions. [ISO 13194:2011](https://www.iso.org/standard/53478.html) addresses box-pallet performance. Your parts, inserts and packing arrangement also need to be checked under the planned conditions. After the supplier confirms the limits, arrange a controlled check with representative goods and the intended handling equipment. Inspect whether the stack seats on its intended supports and whether the box or lid presses on the contents.

Send these details with your request for a [pallet box quotation](/contact/). If the loaded stack is confirmed for floor storage only, plan the shipment with a separately confirmed arrangement.
