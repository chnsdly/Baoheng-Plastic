A pallet box can fit the goods yet be difficult to move between workstations. Compare **box-mounted casters, a separate dolly and fork handling** against the same loaded route. A wheeled option adds a box-to-wheel or box-to-dolly interface; a forklift or pallet truck requires an approved entry and enough space. No option is automatically safer or available for every box.

The pictured box has no visible casters or dolly. The image does not establish that these accessories are available or approved for it.

## Choose around the actual route

Record the box model, footprint and base, goods, full mass and distribution, travel distance, frequency, floor surface, gradients, thresholds, turns, pedestrian traffic, parking and transfer points. Box-mounted casters may reduce separate equipment transfers if the supplier approves that configuration. A separate dolly may be useful when the box must also travel without wheels, but the box must seat and remain retained on that exact dolly. Fork handling may suit routes with suitable equipment and trained operators, but requires safe entry, visibility and manoeuvring space.

Ask each supplier for the supported contact areas, wheel or dolly configuration, parking controls, allowed loading and moving conditions, and instructions for the exact revision. Do not drill the box, improvise attachments, tow or lift a combined unit without the responsible suppliers' approval. [Little Giant's pallet dolly](https://www.littlegiant-usa.com/product/pallet-dolly-with-fork-pockets/) illustrates a specific design with fork pockets and optional floor locks; it is not evidence of compatibility with a Baoheng box.

## Check the combined envelope and load

For an example only, contents weigh **300 kg**, the empty box **45 kg** and the separate dolly **35 kg**: the moving assembly is **380 kg**. If a doorway clearance is **1050 mm** and the widest part of the loaded assembly is **1000 mm**, the difference is **50 mm total**, not per side. Handles, wheel swing, tolerances and positioning may reduce it. These numbers are not ratings, safe handling limits or proof of clearance. Confirm separately what each supplier's load limit includes, support and distribution, centre of gravity and the site's permissible route.

Check whether the dolly raises the box above a door, work surface or stack height, whether handles or guards interfere, and whether the box can move relative to the platform. A caster rating alone does not approve the assembly, floor, slope or stacking arrangement. Parking brakes or floor locks do not replace a site assessment; ask how they operate and where parking is permitted.

## Approve and trial the whole sequence

Have the site safety team and equipment owners agree the sample, representative safe load, transfer method, route, parking, stop criteria and observers. Under their approved procedure, observe loading onto the dolly or fork entry, starting, turning, stopping, parking, unloading and empty return. Include real floor transitions and normal peak frequency. Stop for slipping, instability, damage, uncontrolled movement, obstructed brakes or unsafe effort; do not force the load or bypass protection. UK [HSE push/pull guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/msd/pushpull/index.htm) treats the work as a risk assessment, not a universal box-weight limit.

Keep the accepted box and accessory revisions, total mass, route and exclusions with the order. Reassess changes to wheels, load, floor, equipment or method.
