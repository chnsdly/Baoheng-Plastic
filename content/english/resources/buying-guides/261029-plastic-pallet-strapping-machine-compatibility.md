Replacing a wooden pallet with plastic can change the path available to an automatic strapping machine. A runner may block the guide, a strap may tighten against an unsupported deck edge, or the finished loop may sit where a pallet jack needs to enter. Check these contacts before a volume order.

Whether replacing wooden pallets or changing a plastic pallet model, ask: **where will each strap pass, and what will it bear against after tensioning?** Confirm that path with the machine supplier, then test the exact pallet with the actual goods. A footprint and a load rating do not describe the strapping interface.

## Draw the complete strap loop

Mark the proposed loop on a top, side and underside view of the pallet and load. Show whether the strap only bundles the goods or connects them to the pallet, where it crosses the deck, and where the joint will sit. If the line uses both longitudinal and crosswise straps, draw both paths and the orientation at each station.

This drawing prevents a common misunderstanding: a recess that keeps a loose strap in position may not be a passage for a machine's moving guide. The guide must enter and withdraw; the strap must feed, pull back, tighten and release from the guide without catching. Each movement needs space in the actual machine arrangement.

[Signode's MH-P information](https://www.signode.com/productslist/mhp/) lists an underfeed chute and a pallet-void feeding attachment as separate options. The purchasing implication is to identify the fitted equipment rather than assume that every pallet strapping machine follows the same lower path.

## Four-way fork entry does not define a strap channel

A fork opening that accepts handling equipment is not automatically aligned with a strapping guide. Ask the equipment supplier for the guide's full movement envelope, entry height, position and clearance requirements. Compare those with the pallet drawing, including runners, feet, ribs and any reinforcement near the intended path.

Check the narrowest point along the whole passage, not just the visible opening. The relationship can change when the pallet is turned or when the loaded deck deflects. A drawing review should therefore include the support arrangement at the strapping station and the expected loaded condition.

Do not specify a universal slot width from a photograph. Let the machine supplier define the required clearance and let the pallet supplier identify the relevant dimensions and tolerances for the quoted model. If the path is obstructed, resolve the pallet choice or equipment arrangement before ordering. Cutting a channel into a sample would change the product being evaluated.

For roller or chain lines, use the separate [conveyor bottom-contact checks](/resources/insights/2640-plastic-pallet-conveyor-bottom-contact-checks/) to confirm support and transfer. Smooth conveyor travel alone does not prove that the strap guide can pass.

## Choose tension with the load, strap and pallet together

The machine setting, the strap's breaking force and the pallet's stated load rating describe different things. None gives a universal strapping tension for a finished unit load. The packaging team needs to select a combination that holds the goods without unacceptable carton compression, pallet distortion or strap damage.

[FROMM's consumables overview](https://www.fromm-pack.co.uk/strapping-solutions/strapping-consumables) distinguishes PET and PP strapping applications. That is a starting point for selection; confirm the exact strap grade, width, thickness and joint method with the equipment and consumables suppliers. Record those details alongside the trial settings so a later strap substitution is visible.

Inspect where the tensioned strap turns around the goods and pallet. It may press on a carton edge, a deck lip or a narrow rib rather than a broad supported surface. [FROMM's corner-protector guidance](https://www.fromm-pack.co.uk/strapping-solutions/strapping-consumables/pallet-corner-protectors-edgeboards/plastic-corner-protectors) identifies edge protection as a way to reduce strap cutting into cargo. Trial the chosen protector in its intended position; its presence does not establish a suitable tension or protect every pallet contact point.

If the machine also compresses the load from above, record the compression setting separately from strap tension. Check the complete arrangement after the compression device releases and after the agreed waiting period, when packaging may have settled. A neat-looking loop immediately after sealing is only one observation.

## Follow the strapped pallet beyond the machine

Trace the finished strap through the next conveyor, turn, transfer, fork entry and storage position. Check whether it hangs below a runner, crosses a rolling-contact surface, covers a sensor target or can be scraped by the handling equipment. Resolve interference through an approved routing or equipment change, rather than by leaving a loose loop.

Where the load is also stretch-wrapped, agree the order of operations. Film, protectors and strap joints must work together without hiding a failed joint or blocking the guide. The [stretch-wrapper compatibility guide](/resources/insights/2636-plastic-pallet-stretch-wrapper-compatibility/) covers the separate film-to-pallet checks.

## Turn the trial into an acceptance record

The responsible equipment team should run the trial under the machine manufacturer's operating and safety instructions. Agree the pallet revision, load range, packaging, strap specification, allowed orientations and acceptance criteria first.

1. Check the pallet geometry and guide movement using the equipment supplier's approved method.
2. Run representative loaded units, including the lightest or most compression-sensitive pack and the heaviest approved load where relevant.
3. Observe feed, pullback, tensioning, joint formation, guide withdrawal and discharge. Record misfeeds, rubbing, stops and manual corrections.
4. Inspect the strap, joint, protectors, cartons and pallet after release, the agreed dwell period and downstream handling.
5. Retain the accepted drawings, settings, observations and sample identity for repeat orders.

Set sample quantities and repeat cycles from the line's actual variation with the responsible team. A cycle requiring an unplanned manual correction needs investigation before it is accepted. Connect this record to the broader [pallet load-test plan](/resources/insights/2616-plastic-pallet-load-test-before-bulk-order/) when storage or transport performance is also being qualified. Strapping-line acceptance covers only the conditions actually checked.

## What a useful pallet inquiry contains

Send the machine model and fitted guide arrangement, a marked strap-loop drawing, pallet orientation, unit-load dimensions and mass, carton or pack type, strap specification, compression if used, downstream equipment and trial requirements. A clear underside photograph helps explain the current pallet, but does not replace dimensions.
