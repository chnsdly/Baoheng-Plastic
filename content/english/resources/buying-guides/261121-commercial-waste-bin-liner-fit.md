A bag labelled for the same number of litres as a wheeled bin can still slip inside, hang above the bottom or prevent the lid from closing. **First establish whether the collection service accepts a liner, then measure the actual bin and trial the bag in the agreed emptying process.** This guide covers ordinary commercial waste; hazardous, clinical and sharp waste require a separate competent assessment.

The picture shows closed bins, not a liner or an approved bag-and-bin combination. Liner supply and any special fittings must be confirmed for the order.

## Agree what the collector will accept

Record the site, waste stream, collector, collection point and current instruction. Ask whether the waste must be loose or bagged, which liner materials, colours and sizes are accepted, whether the full-size bin liner may be presented with its contents, and whether bags must be closed. Acceptance of a small kitchen caddy liner does not automatically extend to a full-bin liner. A compostable or biodegradable claim alone does not establish acceptance at the receiving facility. [Ecopack's 240 L liner information](https://www.ecopack.com.au/collections/bin-liners) makes this size and service distinction for its own product.

If liners are prohibited, do not substitute a different bag without approval; consider an unlined bin and the agreed cleaning route. If they are permitted, ask the liner supplier for its measurement convention, material, seam, dimensions, intended load and handling instructions. Do not infer a bag's strength from thickness or nominal litres alone.

## Measure the opening and the path to the bottom

Measure around the outside of the rim where the bag must fold over, avoiding hinges and protrusions where appropriate. Also measure internal height, bottom shape, corners, lid clearance and the room needed for overhang and any closure. Check the actual model: equal nominal capacities do not guarantee equal openings.

For an illustrative **ungusseted** bag, a measured rim circumference of **2000 mm** gives a preliminary flat opening width of **2000 ÷ 2 = 1000 mm**. A candidate stated by its supplier as **1100 mm usable flat opening width** gives 2200 mm around before allowances. For an internal height of **950 mm**, a provisional **250 mm** base allowance and **100 mm** overhang give a starting length of **1300 mm**. These are assumed measurements and allowances, not a Baoheng model specification, a universal sizing rule or a guaranteed fit.

Confirm how the supplier measures width, gussets, seams and length: folded width is not necessarily the usable opening. Bottom geometry, taper, fastening, lid and closure can change the allowance; do not add it twice. [Berry's liner selection guide](https://s3.amazonaws.com/imperialdade.com/apps/catalog/digital-assets/106340/product-documentation/9716e42c3ad32157f0fd9cc6e54495d30981d492.pdf) describes circumference-based width as a starting point. Request a sample when the dimensions or definition remain uncertain.

## Trial the complete handover

Use the proposed bin, lid and liner with a representative, safely prepared load and the collector's agreed method. Fit the bag so it reaches the bottom without tension, stays at the rim, does not cover handles or lifting points, and permits the lid to close. Check whether it snags on corners, fasteners or the emptying equipment. Observe the agreed closure and emptying: does the bag release with the contents, remain in the bin, or need separate removal? The answer must match the service instruction.

Check tears, leaks, trapped residue, bag movement and the condition of the lid and lifting interface. Stop the trial and have the responsible site team review it if the bag tears, the bin cannot close, the collection mechanism is obstructed or safe handling cannot be maintained. A trial shows only the tested combination; it does not prove all loads or repeated cycles. Do not manually lift a full large liner unless that operation and its load have been specifically assessed and approved.

## Put the decision into the purchasing record

Keep the stream and collector instruction; bin model, rim circumference and internal height; supplier's bag dimensions and measurement convention; allowed material and presentation; sample outcome; and the person approving changes. Recheck when the bin, liner, contents, collector or emptying method changes.
