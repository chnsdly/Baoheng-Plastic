If an automated storage and retrieval system (AS/RS) rejects a loaded plastic pallet at infeed, **get the reject code and the station's acceptance drawing before buying a replacement**. A shifted load, a damaged runner and a failed label read can all send a pallet to the reject lane. Each calls for a different action. Use the site's approved process to hold and inspect the load.

At [CeMAT ASIA 2026 in Shanghai](https://www.comau.com/en/2026/11/05/comau-and-automha-showcase-warehouse-and-intralogistics-automation-at-cemat-asia-2026/), Automha presented its multi-deep automated pallet storage system. The infeed question is separate from shuttle selection. In [Mecalux's Grupo Siro installation](https://www.mecalux.com/clients/example-clad-rack-cold-storage-grupo-siro-spain), an entry checkpoint checks pallet condition, weight and dimensions, then routes failed loads according to the anomaly. Checkpoints and reject rules vary by installation. The cover illustration shows a storage lane, not either installation's inspection station.

## Decide what needs to change

- **Profile or weight reject.** Record the greatest loaded length, width and height, gross weight and travel direction, including bulging cartons, lean, film and corner boards. Ask which axis, side or limit triggered the reject. Repack or recenter under the site procedure if the load is the cause. Compare actual pallet dimensions with the current drawing if the pallet itself is out of tolerance.
- **Base or condition reject.** Once the load is safe to inspect, check the required runner positions, openings and contact surfaces against the drawing. Isolate a cracked pallet or one with broken supports. If intact pallets of the same model repeatedly fail the base check, confirm the orientation and required geometry with the integrator before changing the purchase specification.
- **Identification or positioning reject.** Reconcile the label and warehouse record, and confirm the load was presented at the correct position and orientation. If conforming loads fail at the same station, ask the integrator to review the events and equipment. Use the [sensor detection guide](/resources/insights/2633-plastic-pallet-sensor-detection-automation/) to investigate recurring read failures.

## Measure the loaded width on both sides

For a simple rectangular load, measure at the outermost points in the station's width direction. A pallet measuring **1000 mm** with the finished load extending **12 mm** beyond the left edge and **8 mm** beyond the right edge occupies **1020 mm** overall. Against a **hypothetical 1015 mm** limit, it exceeds the limit by **5 mm**. Use the station drawing for its reference points and permitted offsets. A load can fit the total width and still fail a separate limit on one side. These figures illustrate the calculation only.

Do not put a damaged pallet back on the line to repeat the fault. A site-approved slave pallet can be considered for a sound but incompatible carrier only if the system permits that arrangement. Check the combined height, gross weight, stability, support and transfer points, then repeat the agreed entry check. See the [pallet shuttle selection guide](/resources/buying-guides/260810-pallet-shuttle-interface-selection/) for direct entry and slave-pallet arrangements. Passing the checkpoint remains one step in approval for the full loaded route.

## Prepare a replacement pallet inquiry

If evidence points to the pallet, send Baoheng the checkpoint drawing and revision, reject code, pallet size and direction, underside photos or drawing, completed load dimensions and mass, rack or conveyor support details and order quantity. Ask for a model drawing and a sample to compare with a known passing load on the agreed route before bulk purchase.
