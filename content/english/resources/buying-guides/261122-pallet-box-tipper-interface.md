A pallet box that fits a forklift can still be unsuitable for a machine that lifts and tips it into a hopper. **Before ordering, obtain the equipment owner's approved interface and ask the box supplier to confirm the exact box and loaded tipping conditions.** Dimensions alone do not approve retention, structural contact or discharge.

The photograph shows a box with its side door closed, not a tipper or an approved tipping configuration. It does not establish that this model is suitable for tipping.

## Define the job and the responsible approvals

Record the contents, whether they flow freely or stick, their maximum loaded mass and distribution, any liner or lid, the emptying rate and the receiving hopper. Identify the exact tipper model and revision, how the box enters, and who approves the equipment, box and load. Avoid inferring direct food-contact suitability, dust control or other special performance without the applicable evidence.

The [GKS T-500 description](https://www.gk-sondermaschinenbau.com/en/lifting-tipping-devices/) lists a bin-adapted frame, centring and retaining flaps for that machine. [POSIMAT's box tipper](https://www.posimat.com/en/other-solutions/box-tipper/posimat-box-tipper) describes a different enclosed hopper-loading setup. These examples show why the installed machine's documentation matters; neither approves a Baoheng box.

## Match the box to support and retention

Ask the equipment owner for its current drawing, permitted container envelope, approach direction, support surfaces, restraint locations, mass definition, motion envelope and operating instructions. Ask the box supplier for the exact revision, external dimensions and tolerances, base and runner geometry, wall and rim features, doors, hinges, latches and the contact and tipping conditions it can support. A closed door or an upright static-load rating does not establish that the box can withstand tipped contents or restraint forces.

Compare the drawings for interference at every contact point. A lip or latch that prevents seating, a restraint that presses on an unapproved wall or a liner that can catch at discharge is an unresolved interface, not a minor dimensional issue. Do not improvise straps, holes, adapters or machine settings: have the equipment owner and box supplier approve any proposed solution.

## Check clearance and mass without treating arithmetic as approval

For illustration, an equipment opening is **1250 mm** wide and the box is **1200 mm** wide at the relevant height and orientation. The difference is **50 mm total**, not 50 mm on each side. Hinges, tolerances, alignment and equipment movement may consume that space. The drawing and responsible owner decide whether clearance is adequate.

In a separate example, contents of **380 kg**, an empty box of **60 kg** and a liner or removable insert of **20 kg** total **460 kg**. A machine limit of **500 kg** leaves a numerical difference of **40 kg**, provided the equipment owner confirms all three components are included in the same limit. The values are examples, not ratings. They do not establish allowable centre of gravity, sidewall force, tipping speed, safe clearance or a usable safety margin.

## Review discharge and run an approved trial

Check the full swept path against building structure, adjacent equipment, guards and the hopper. Agree the discharge side, outlet position, dust or spillage controls, product-flow behaviour and whether the hopper can receive the actual surge. Confirm liner and lid handling with both suppliers. If flow stalls, never reach into moving equipment or bypass a guard; follow the site and equipment owner's isolation and intervention procedures.

Only after the responsible owners approve the intended configuration and safeguarding, use a representative box, load and hopper in a controlled trial under their procedures. Record seating, restraint contact, box movement or distortion, lid and latch condition, discharge and residual material. Stop for unexpected movement, damage, obstruction, spillage outside the controlled area or any safeguarding fault. One trial applies only to its documented conditions; reapprove changes to the box, load, restraint, machine, hopper or process.

## Send a complete inquiry

Send the box and machine drawings and revisions, load and total mass, orientation, support and restraint contact, hopper position, environment, intended cycles and approval owners. Request evidence for the exact quoted box rather than a general “tipper compatible” claim.
