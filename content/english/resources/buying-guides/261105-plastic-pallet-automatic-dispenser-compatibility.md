An empty plastic pallet can fit inside the dispenser’s magazine and still bring a second pallet with it on release. If you are replacing pallets on an automated line, approve the exact pallet design and dispenser setup together. The sample has to be held at the right points, separate cleanly from the stack and arrive on the next conveyor in the correct position.

## Mark where the dispenser holds the stack

In a bottom-feed dispenser, the machine holds the remaining stack while releasing the lowest pallet. A rib can obstruct a retaining finger, or the available gap can be too narrow for the finger to enter. The dimensions that matter are at the contact points and along the gripper's path. Top-pick machines need their own contact and clearance checks.

For an existing line, start with the dispenser model, the approved settings and a pallet that already runs. Ask the equipment supplier or integrator to mark whether it grips the long or short side, the contact surfaces and the required clearances on a drawing. Have the pallet supplier show those areas on side and underside views, with tolerances and the exact feet or runners installed. For a new line, agree on the same details before approving the machine layout. PALOMAT's enquiry form shows the level of detail involved. It asks for a pallet drawing, the gripping direction and the conveyor dimensions. [PALOMAT Inline enquiry form](https://www.palomat.com/media/tsubrpt1/enquiry_form_inline_uk_aug22-1.pdf)

## Measure how much height each empty pallet adds

On a nestable pallet, the hollow feet enter recesses in the pallet below. The **nesting pitch** is the increase in stack height when you add another pallet. Measure the height of one pallet and of two nested pallets on a level surface. Subtract the first measurement from the second, then verify the height and alignment of a full stack in the intended orientation. [Cabka's description of nestable pallets](https://cabka.com/pallets/features-nestable)

Suppose two designs both measure **1,200 × 1,000 × 150 mm**. Ten pallets of design A, stacked without overlap, stand **1,500 mm** high. If each additional pallet of design B adds **60 mm** when nested, ten stand **150 + 9 × 60 = 690 mm** high. This example assumes consistent spacing throughout each stack.

The shorter stack needs less storage height, but its smaller gaps or deeper overlap may require a different gripping or separation mechanism. Give the integrator the measured stack height, pallet count and total empty-stack mass. Check each against the dispenser limits, then confirm that the machine can grip and separate that specific stack. Innoveyance, for example, lists stack-height range, magazine capacity and maximum stack weight separately. [Innoveyance dispenser specifications](https://innoveyance.com/product/pallet-conveyor-system/)

## Watch the pallet reach the first conveyor

A pallet can separate cleanly and still rock, turn or catch at the first transfer. Check where its feet or runners sit on the receiving rollers or chains, especially across the gap between the dispenser and conveyor. Use the real discharge direction. Verify that the station’s sensors register the empty pallet in position and that the receiving position clears before another pallet is released. [Plastic pallet bottom-contact checks](/resources/insights/2640-plastic-pallet-conveyor-bottom-contact-checks/)

## Agree on the sample trial before ordering

Define a successful feed as one request delivering **exactly one pallet**, within the agreed time and in the required position and orientation, without manual help. With the line integrator, choose the run length, required feed rate and allowable faults before testing.

- Run representative production samples from a full magazine through the last pallet. Include routine replenishment and a restart after a normal stop.
- If the line uses returned pallets, include serviceable returns within the agreed wear and dimensional limits. Keep pallet designs and configurations separate unless the line is intended to run them mixed.
- Log every request and any missed feed, double feed, skew, jam or intervention. Run the trial on the actual transfer with the normal guards in place and the integrator's approved procedure.

Record the approved pallet revision, orientation and machine settings together. A change to the feet, runners or gripping surfaces calls for a fresh check of that interface.
