Shortlist a drop-door pallet box when operators pick parts one at a time and the full-height wall gets in the way. Opening the side door lowers the edge each part must clear. The door earns its space at the station only if the part and the operator's grip fit through the opening, the door can swing clear, and the operator can reach the last part at the bottom.

For a station that lifts parts out from above with a hoist, or where a lower box already gives good top access, start with a box without a side door. If the last pick still requires reaching deep across the box, compare a shallower carrier, smaller totes, or a suitable positioning aid. Use the same parts and pick sequence for each comparison.

## Measure the opening and the space around it

Put the sample in the position it will occupy at the station. Mark which side faces the operator and which side the forklift or pallet truck will use to place the box. An opening on the far side is of little use when a fixture or wall keeps the operator from reaching it. Ask the supplier to mark the door side and opening dimensions on the drawing for the quoted revision. Product sheets can list the opening separately from the outside and inside box dimensions. [ORBIS BulkPak GP4048-34 specification, February 2024](https://www.orbiscorporation.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ORB_BulkPak-GP4048_Spec-Sheet-2024-0213.pdf)

1. **Clear opening.** Measure the narrowest usable width along the part's removal path with the door fully open. Account for latches, tapered sides and any liner or insert. Check the available height if a lid, shelf or fixture limits upward movement. Include the part's normal grip and any tray removed with it.
2. **Sill and pick height.** The sill is the lower edge of the opening. Measure from the operator's floor to the sill, and from the part's resting surface to that same edge. The second distance shows how far a low part must rise before it can pass out.
3. **Door swing and standing space.** Mark the space the door passes through from closed to fully open. Check it against the bench, adjacent bins and stops. With the door open, check whether the operator can stand close enough to grip the part and transfer it to the work surface.

**Example.** A part and its tray span **450 mm** at their widest point and must come out level and facing forward. A **420 mm** clear opening is **30 mm too narrow** (450 − 420). A **480 mm** opening leaves **30 mm total** (480 − 450) before allowing for hands or dimensional variation. Keep the second option on the shortlist and check it with the loaded sample. These are example dimensions.

## Trial the first pick and the last pick

Agree with the supplier and the site's handling lead on how the box will be supported and which loaded door operations are allowed. Check how the pack keeps parts clear of the door during opening. If contents can press against the door, hold the trial until the supplier and site have reviewed the pack and opening method.

With the intended gloves, dunnage and workstation layout, check the first pick from the normal full pack, the widest part in its removal orientation, and the last pick from the farthest occupied position. Watch the whole transfer to the work surface. Stop if a part catches or shifts toward the opening, the door or latch binds, or an operator has to lean on the open door or improvise a grip. The site's handling lead should assess the actual reach, posture, part weight and repetition before release. [UK HSE manual-handling assessment guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/msd/manual-handling/assess-manual-handling.htm)

Record the box revision, door orientation, pack arrangement and workstation setup that passed the review. If the boxes fold for return, check the [empty-box folding and handling sequence](/resources/buying-guides/260815-foldable-pallet-box-manual-handling-check/) separately.

## Request a comparable quote and sample

Send the largest part or tray dimensions, part and loaded box masses, the pack layout, pick frequency, and a station sketch showing the operator side, delivery direction and nearest obstruction. Ask each supplier for a drawing of the quoted door configuration with its opening, sill and swing dimensions, any restrictions on operating the door with contents in the box, and a sample of that configuration.
