RISK Common mismatches

What usually goes wrong

  • Racking load is quoted without beam span or deflection expectation
  • Forklift impact shortens pallet life in high-turnover aisles
  • One-way export pallets are confused with repeat-use warehouse pallets
  • Empty return cost is ignored until the pallet program scales

SELECT Key factors

Details to confirm before quoting

Storage mode

Floor storage, beam racking, drive-in racks, and temporary staging require different base structures.

Load behavior

Uniform cartons, bags, drums, and point loads behave differently even at the same weight.

Handling equipment

Forklift, pallet jack, conveyor, and automation requirements affect entry direction and tolerance.
