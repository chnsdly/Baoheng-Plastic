Production and quality control

See how we manufacture →
01 / MATERIAL

Materials and product construction

We review HDPE or PP, impact-modified material, recycled content, anti-slip surfaces, steel reinforcement, and color or logo requirements against the intended use.

02 / MOLDING

Molding and tooling

Injection and blow molding cover our standard models. For suitable volume orders, tooling can be adjusted for ribs, deck texture, label areas, and reinforcement.

03 / RELEASE

Inspection, packing, and export

Batch checks, visual inspection, stacking tests, packing plans, shipment photos, and container loading records help you verify the order before it leaves the factory.
