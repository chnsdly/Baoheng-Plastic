02 / Eight comparison fields

A series page should make differences visible.

The production version can use verified content fields to compare models without turning the website into an automatic engineering approval tool.

01 — FOOTPRINT

Dimensions

External length, width and height plus unit-load overhang.

02 — BASE

Support geometry

Runners, legs, perimeter or reversible contact.

03 — ENTRY

Equipment access

Fork direction, openings and pallet-jack constraints.

04 — STORAGE

Support mode

Floor, stack, beam rack, conveyor or automation.

05 — CONDITIONS

Load & environment

Distribution, duration, temperature and exposure.
